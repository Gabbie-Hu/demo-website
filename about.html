About Me

Hi! I'm a student at NYU majoring in Ecnomics and Mathematics. I’ve always loved building things, and I’m especially interested in how design and code come together to create user-friendly, beautiful websites.

This portfolio is a collection of my work from a web design course, where I’ve explored HTML, CSS, responsive layouts, JavaScript interactivity, and visual aesthetics. I enjoy blending function with style — whether through clean layouts, soft colors, or playful details.

Outside of class, I enjoy K-pop, photo editing, and learning new digital tools. I'm excited to keep growing my skills and using web design as a creative space.
